How PUSH AI builds

Speed is cheap now.
Discipline is the product.

Every agency now says it uses AI. Google's 2025 study of nearly 5 000 software teams found AI makes them ship faster — and break more. "AI doesn't fix a team; it amplifies what's already there." So the method matters more than the tool. Ours has four steps, and you can see where you are in every one of them.

01

Scope

A free 90-minute conversation about where your week goes. We write down, in plain language, what the system must do — your words, not ours — with a price and a date. It's yours to keep, whether or not you build with us.

02

Build

We build in short rounds of about two weeks. Before each round you approve exactly what it will deliver; at the end you see it working. AI writes most of the code; a person reads every change before it goes anywhere near your live system.

03

Verify

"It works on my screen" doesn't count. We check the real data, on the real system, with your people using it, before anything is called done — and we show you the evidence.

04

Hand over

Live on the date we named, with your team trained on it. The code, the logins and the documentation are yours — no lock-in. And because the system records what happens from day one, the next thing you need builds on top, not from scratch.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What do you actually build?

Real production systems that solve a business problem: operations and work tracking, quoting, stock, bookings, enquiry capture, customer records, online stores, client portals, reporting. Any industry — workshops, dealerships, retail, clinics, agencies, logistics, professional services. The first build is the one costing you the most hours, delivered end to end, with the logging in place so the next one plugs in without a rewrite.

What does it cost?

It's scoped and priced in writing in the free session, before anything is built — so you know the number up front. Because the build is AI-augmented and there's no agency overhead to carry, PUSH AI comes in 20–30% below traditional agency pricing, with faster delivery.

How long does it take?

First builds are scoped small enough to ship in weeks, not quarters. The date comes from real engineering capacity, not sales pressure — and once it's confirmed to you, it's kept.

Who actually does the work?

A small, senior-led practice. Len scopes your system, gates every change before it goes live, and answers the phone afterwards. AI development tooling does the heavy lifting under that gate — no account manager between you and the person accountable for the build.

Do I own what you build?

Everything. Code, repository, credentials and handover documentation are yours. If we part ways, you take all of it with you.

You use AI to build it — is that safe?

AI does the heavy lifting. A delivery lead with twenty years behind him reviews every change before it reaches your live system, and nothing is called done until the database and real data say so. "Done means done, verified with evidence." Speed from the AI, safety from the gate.

What about load-shedding and patchy signal?

Systems are built to keep working on a phone with unreliable signal, to notify your team over WhatsApp, and to run on hosting chosen for reliability rather than fashion.
